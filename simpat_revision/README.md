# Reproducibility supplement for the SIMPAT submission (v2.0.0)

This directory accompanies *Reference-Calibrated Selective Verification of
Surrogate Design Jacobians under Finite Simulation Budgets*. It separates
frozen-result replay, optional surrogate retraining, numerical verification of
reference labels, and device-level forward-response evidence. These evidence
tiers must not be conflated.

`evidence_artifact_manifest.csv` records the SHA-256 digest and byte size of
the frozen inputs, full-wave logs, and component-level verification table used
by this supplement.

## Evidence boundary

- The reference-labelled quantitative claims use only the two transfer-matrix
  benchmarks and the heat/Poisson benchmark: 3,580 gradient components in
  total.
- All 3,580 reference signs pass the declared step-size/independent-derivative
  screen. The heat benchmark additionally passes the 160/320/640-cell sign
  check. See `reference_gradient_verification.md` and the three
  `reference_gradient_*.csv` files.
- The openEMS antenna runs do **not** provide reference-gradient labels. All six
  zones are positive at `h=0.025` and `h=0.05` but negative at `h=0.10` on the
  documented 189 x 189 x 78 mesh. No mesh-refinement result is available.
  Therefore the antenna material is limited to response-level corroboration
  and is excluded from AUC, risk, calibration, gradient-error, cost-saving, and
  allocator claims.
- The private end-to-end device PINN training framework is not bundled. Device
  figure arrays are frozen-output redraw inputs, not a from-scratch training
  release.

## 1. Frozen-result replay (no training and no reference-simulator call)

The following files reproduce the revision analyses from frozen arrays:

- `analyze_external_baselines.py`: head-to-head mean-aligned sign, SNR, and
  magnitude comparisons; query-cluster bootstrap intervals; matched-coverage
  operating points; fixed 50/50 query split; and finite-budget correction.
- `external_members_*.npz`: validated per-member gradients for the three
  external benchmarks.
- `external_baseline_*.csv`, `external_modal_mean_disagreement.csv`, and
  `snr_external_baselines.md`: tabulated outputs and audit report.
- `fig_external_score_comparison.py` and `fig_external_score_comparison.pdf`:
  the main score-comparison figure.
- `risk_coverage_simpat.py`, the three `extbench_*.npz` files, and
  `fig_risk_coverage.pdf`: threshold-0.9 risk--coverage and exact central-FD
  cost accounting.
- `toy_model_simpat.py` and `fig_toy_simpat.pdf`: fixed-seed scale-coupling and
  shared-bias theory illustration.
- `graphical_abstract_simpat.py`: Matplotlib source for the graphical abstract,
  with PDF, SVG, and high-resolution PNG outputs.

From this directory, with Python 3.11 or newer:

```bash
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python analyze_external_baselines.py --analysis-dir . --package-dir .
python risk_coverage_simpat.py --data-dir . --out-dir .
python reference_gradient_verification.py --data-dir . --out-dir .
python fig_external_score_comparison.py
python toy_model_simpat.py
python graphical_abstract_simpat.py
```

The **calibration results cited by the manuscript** are generated by
`analyze_external_baselines.py` and stored in
`external_baseline_split_calibration.csv`. They use candidate intended
coverages of 10%, ..., 100%, with a Bonferroni-adjusted calibration screen over
the ten thresholds within each predeclared score. The adjustment does not
cover post hoc selection among scores; the deployment score must be
predeclared from the intended loss or selected with an additional
multiplicity-aware protocol.

`split_calibration_candidates.csv` and `split_calibration_selection.csv` are
older, sign-only exploratory outputs emitted by `risk_coverage_simpat.py`.
They are retained for auditability but are **not** the manuscript's primary
per-score calibration evidence.

Both analysis scripts use 10,000 query-cluster bootstrap replicates and seed
`20260711`; the allocator uses 512 truth-blind random tie breaks. One central
finite-difference component check is charged as two reference-objective
evaluations.

## 2. Optional reconstruction of the per-member artifacts (retraining)

`rebuild_external_member_gradients.py` replays the archived external benchmark
definitions and fixed training seeds. Unlike the commands in Section 1, this
step **does retrain** the small external surrogate ensembles. It writes an
`external_members_*.npz` artifact only after exact reproduction of the frozen
`all_sa` and `all_correct` arrays and numerical agreement with the archived AUC
and zero-budget cosine. The accepted hashes and shapes are recorded in
`external_member_gradient_manifest.csv`.

The AUC field in that reconstruction manifest is the archived modal-vote
quantity used only as a checksum against the v1 frozen generators. It is not
the v2 operational score table. Current mean-aligned-sign, SNR, and magnitude
results are in `external_baseline_auc.csv` and are generated by
`analyze_external_baselines.py`.

```bash
python rebuild_external_member_gradients.py --dataset all \
  --frozen-dir . --out-dir rebuilt_members
```

The original benchmark generators (`extbench_tmm.py`,
`extbench_tmm_hard.py`, and `extbench_poisson.py`) are also included for source
provenance. Retraining is not required to reproduce any reported table or
figure because the validated member-gradient artifacts are supplied.

## 3. Reference-gradient numerical verification

`reference_gradient_verification.py` does not retrain a surrogate. It
reconstructs the exact benchmark queries and verifies the frozen central-FD
labels against:

- an analytic characteristic-matrix derivative for both TMM benchmarks; and
- a complex-step derivative through the discrete heat solve, plus a
  160/320/640-cell grid audit.

Outputs are:

- `reference_gradient_verification_components.csv` (all 3,580 components),
- `reference_gradient_verification_summary.csv`,
- `reference_gradient_label_metrics.csv`, and
- `reference_gradient_verification.md`.

The verification retains every frozen component, so the frozen AUC and
threshold-0.9 risk/coverage values are unchanged.

## 4. openEMS step-ladder provenance (expensive; device gradients unresolved)

The submitted frozen records are:

- `openems_step_ladder_raw.csv` and `openems_step_ladder_h10.csv`: completed
  six-zone `h=0.025,0.05,0.10` checkpoints, including an independent repeat at
  `h=0.10`;
- `grad_fullwave.npz`: the released nominal `h=0.05` baseline and six-zone
  objective responses used by the step-ladder summarizer;
- `openems_step_ladder.log` and `openems_step_ladder_h10.log`: solver logs;
- `openems_mesh_inventory.csv`: mesh dimensions parsed from those logs;
- `openems_step_ladder_summary.csv`: declared numerical-resolution screen;
- `openems_step_ladder_h10_repeatability.csv`: cross-run sign and numerical
  comparison; and
- `openems_variant_one_sided_diagnostic.csv`: why the two fixed-step antenna
  variants are exploratory rather than verified gradients.

`openems_step_ladder.py`, `summarize_openems_step_ladder.py`,
`parse_openems_mesh_logs.py`, and
`openems_variant_one_sided_diagnostic.py` provide the corresponding source.
The full-wave rerun requires the complete project tree plus local openEMS and
CSXCAD Python bindings; these are not installed by `requirements.txt` and are
not needed to inspect or replay the controlled benchmark results. A successful
step-size audit would still not constitute mesh convergence; the supplied audit
in fact fails because every zone changes sign at `h=0.10`.

## 5. Figure redraw provenance

`figure_revisions/` contains deterministic redraw scripts, frozen inputs, and
vector/raster outputs. The current controlled-only hybrid figure deliberately
omits a device-gradient panel. Historical reliability/refutation redraws that
are not cited by the current manuscript remain in the local working tree but
are deliberately excluded from the submission archive; they do not add
claims.

## Archive status

This directory is the authoritative v2.0.0 revision supplement in GitHub tag
`v2.0.0`. Zenodo archives that release under the all-version concept DOI
<https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21005573> and assigns a separate DOI to the
specific version. The original v1.0.0 archive remains available for provenance
but does not contain the revised reference-label and risk--coverage evidence.
